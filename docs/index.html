HOW TO PLAY

1

ONE MATCH, TWO PHONES

One trainer taps NEW MATCH. The other scans the QR on the lobby screen, or types the 5-letter code. Both phones then show the same battle, so nobody has to trust a scoreboard.

2

TAKE TURNS

Tap a move. It costs 1 PP and drops your rival's HP, then it's their turn. The badge in the top corner tells you whose turn it is.

3

TYPES BEAT EACH OTHER

STUDY › LECTURE › GYM › COMMUTE › SOCIAL › STUDY. Beating a type is 1.5×, and 2.25× if they already have a status. A resisted hit is 0.75×.

4

STATUS AND PP

PARALYSED, BURNOUT, SNOOZE and DOOMSCROLL all bite: lost turns, -5 HP a turn, or -4 HP a turn. SU BAR DECOMP cures everything. Run a move dry and you get a second wind.

5

CALLING CAP · 3 PER MATCH

Think your rival faked a habit? Tap CALL CAP. They have to show proof with the camera, or take the hit: -25 HP and a FRAUD flag.

If the proof lands, the call backfires — you lose 15 HP instead. No jury, no votes: screenshot or it didn't happen.

6

CAM PROOF

CAM PROOF opens the Game Boy camera and shoots 160×144. Point it at the actual evidence: your habit tracker, your notes app, the treadmill screen. The photo lands in the room for both phones.

7

FOCUS DUEL

Both phones get a button. Wait for TAP NOW! then hit it. The slower trainer takes 12 HP and falls asleep.

8

PRINTER

Prints the match as a Game Boy receipt PNG. Straight to the group chat.
