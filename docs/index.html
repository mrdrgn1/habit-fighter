HOW TO PLAY

1

ONE MATCH, TWO PHONES

One trainer taps NEW MATCH. The other scans the QR on the lobby screen, or types the 5-letter code. Both phones then show the same battle.

2

SAME HABIT, THEN LOADOUTS

Only two types exist: STUDY and GYM. Both trainers must pick the same one — a STUDY trainer and a GYM trainer never start a fight. Then take exactly six moves from that type's eight, lock in, and your rival approves it. If the types clash, one tap on SWITCH TO… joins their habit.

3

EVERY MOVE IS A TIMER

No turns and no rounds. Pick a move and you are locked in for its real length — 15 minutes, 2 hours, the whole night. Every move button stays dead until the clock hits zero, then the hit lands and you can pick again. Both phones run their own clock.

4

TIME IS DAMAGE

Damage comes out of the length of the session: 20 minutes is a 17-damage jab, a 2-hour library lock-in 37, an all-nighter 61. Long sessions cost you hours you cannot pick again — that is the trade. Two hours or more on someone already statused is a LOCKED IN combo: 1.5× and it steals 1 🪙.

5

STATUS AND PP

PARALYSED and SNOOZE can eat your session, BURNOUT bites for 5 HP after each one, DOOMSCROLL for 4. Some moves cure you. Run every move dry and you get a second wind.

6

CALLING CAP · 3 PER MATCH

Think your rival faked a habit? Tap CALL CAP. They either show a photo or take the hit: -25 HP and a FRAUD flag, plus 2 🪙 to you.

Nothing verifies a photo. You judge it: accept it and you pay 1 🪙, or reject it and the call stands.

7

CAM PROOF ONLY ANSWERS A CAP

SHOW PROOF is the only thing that opens the Game Boy camera, and only while someone is calling cap on you. No photo, no cap, no camera — that is the whole verification system.

8

FOCUS DUEL · ONCE PER MATCH

Both phones light up on their own. Wait for TAP NOW! then hit it. The slower trainer takes 12 HP and falls asleep.

9

THE WALLET · 50 🪙 ON THIS DEVICE

You start with 50 🪙 and the balance lives on your phone, so it is still there next time. Both sides ante 1, 3 or 5 into the Match Pot; a LOCKED IN combo steals 1 🪙, a busted cap costs the liar 2, a wrong call costs the accuser 1.

Knock them out and you sweep the pot. Hit 0 and you are BANKRUPT: no cap calls left. Every battle is written to BATTLE HISTORY on the title screen. Not real money, just shame.

Walking out mid-match is a forfeit: tap LEAVE twice and you lose the ante sitting in the pot and pay 2 🪙 on top. Your rival takes the whole pot and the match — leaving is always worse than losing.

10

REMATCH AND THE PRINTER

REMATCH only fires when both trainers tap it, and it drops you back to the loadout screen: change your type and picks if you want, then get approved again. The printer turns the match into a Game Boy receipt PNG, token ledger included.
